I am an engineer working on **thermal-fluid and mechanical systems** through hands-on testing, simulation, and data-driven modeling. I am pursuing a **PhD in Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University** in Prof. Hui Hu's **Advanced Flow Diagnostics & Experimental Aerodynamics Lab** and **Aircraft Icing Physics & Anti-/De-Icing Technology Laboratory**.

My work centers on designing and running experiments on rotating machinery and aerodynamic surfaces: building test setups, acquiring force, torque, and power data, measuring flow fields with Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV), and turning experimental data into predictive models. As a teaching assistant, I also lead mechanical testing labs on Instron load frames and torsion, combined-loading, and pressure-vessel test setups.

**Core strengths:** Experimental Fluid Mechanics · Heat Transfer · Aerodynamics · Mechanical Testing · Test & Measurement · Design of Experiments · CFD/FEA · Data Analysis & Machine Learning

I am open to industry, national lab, and research roles in thermal-fluids, test engineering, and data-driven design.
