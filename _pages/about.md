I am a PhD student in **Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University (ISU)**, Ames, Iowa, working in Prof. Hui Hu's research group, which includes the **Advanced Flow Diagnostics & Experimental Aerodynamics Lab** and the **Aircraft Icing Physics & Anti-/De-Icing Technology Laboratory**.

Before ISU, I earned my **B.Sc. in Naval Architecture & Marine Engineering** from the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering at **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)**, ranked 9th of 63 in my class. At BUET I designed a complete 1,700-tonne inland oil tanker, optimized offshore wind farm layouts with a wake-aware genetic algorithm in a RISE-funded thesis, and built machine-learning surrogate models for early-stage ship hydrostatics.

Today my work combines **experiments, simulation, and data**:

- **Experimental testing:** wind tunnel testing, design of experiments, thrust/torque/power measurement, high-speed imaging
- **Flow diagnostics:** mean and phase-locked Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) with LaVision DaVis and Tecplot
- **Mechanical testing:** Instron universal test stations, torsion, combined-loading, and pressure-vessel setups (as a teaching assistant)
- **Simulation and design:** ANSYS Fluent, ABAQUS, AutoCAD, Rhinoceros 3D, Maxsurf
- **Data and optimization:** machine learning, surrogate modeling, genetic algorithms, statistical analysis
- **Prototyping:** structured-light 3D scanning and 3D printing

Outside the lab, I enjoy cricket (I captained my department's tournament-winning team at BUET), badminton, and mobile photography.
