I am a PhD student in **Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University (ISU)** in Ames, Iowa, where I work in the **Advanced Flow Diagnostics & Experimental Aerodynamics Lab** and the **Aircraft Icing Physics & Anti-/De-Icing Technology Laboratory**.

Before ISU, I earned my **B.Sc. in Naval Architecture & Marine Engineering** from the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering at **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)**. At BUET I designed a complete 1,700-tonne inland oil tanker, optimized offshore wind farm layouts with a wake-aware genetic algorithm in a RISE-funded thesis, and built machine-learning surrogate models for early-stage ship hydrostatics.

Today my work brings together **experiments, simulation, and data**: I build and run wind tunnel tests, measure flow fields with Particle Image Velocimetry, and turn experimental data into predictive models. As a teaching assistant, I also lead mechanical testing labs on Instron load frames and torsion, combined-loading, and pressure-vessel setups.

I am open to industry, national lab, and research roles in thermal-fluids, test engineering, and data-driven design. The best way to reach me is by [email](mailto:arif.buet19@gmail.com) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/md-ariful-islam01/).

Outside the lab, I enjoy cricket (I captained my department's tournament-winning team at BUET), badminton, and mobile photography.
